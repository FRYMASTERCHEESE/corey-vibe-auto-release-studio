Commercial-use setup

      
This version creates audio locally with mathematical synthesis and does not use downloaded beats, audio samples, or a paid music-generation API. The project takes 0% of your royalties and claims no ownership share in tracks you create with it.

      
The generated WAV contains ordinary PCM audio only. This version does not use an external generative-music model.

      
No software can guarantee that a newly generated melody will never coincidentally resemble existing music, and copyright/distributor eligibility depends on applicable law and platform rules. See OUTPUT_RIGHTS.md.
