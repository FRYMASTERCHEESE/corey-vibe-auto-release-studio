## Fast Local

The local backing-track engine creates audio procedurally in the browser. Fast Local neural vocals use third-party open-source software/model components. Those third-party components remain under their own licences.

## Studio Singer

When Studio Singer is selected, the website sends the user's prompt and lyrics to the separately configured ACE-Step-compatible server and imports the returned audio into the release workflow. The Corey Vibe website does not own that third-party model and cannot grant rights it does not possess.
