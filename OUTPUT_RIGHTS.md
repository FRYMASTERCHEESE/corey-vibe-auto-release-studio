Corey Vibe Auto Release Studio v4 is software for creating backing tracks, vocal mixes, cover artwork, lyrics and release metadata.

## Neural Singer

When **Neural Singer** is selected, v4 loads third-party open-source software/model weights (listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`) and uses them locally in the browser to generate a voice layer. Those third-party components remain subject to their own licences. This project cannot grant rights it does not own and does not guarantee that a model-generated vocal output qualifies for copyright protection in every jurisdiction.

When **Lightweight synthetic singer** is selected, the vocal tone is generated by the website's own procedural synthesis code.
